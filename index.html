About FoodScan

FoodScan is a free, open-source food barcode scanner that helps you make informed decisions about what you eat. Powered by the Open Food Facts database — the largest open food products database in the world.

✅
Open Data

Built on Open Food Facts, a collaborative project with contributors worldwide.

✅
Privacy First

No data collection. Everything stays on your device.

✅
Always Free

No subscriptions, no ads, no hidden costs — just useful food data.
